You are a senior full-stack engineer and product designer.
Your task is to design and generate the architecture and starter implementation for a **modern mobile learning platform** that teaches IT careers and programming languages.

The application should be similar in concept to learning platforms like SoloLearn and Mimo but expanded to include **IT career paths, certification guidance, coding practice environments, and structured learning paths**.

The platform must be scalable, secure, and designed with a modern minimalistic UI.

---

PROJECT NAME
"TechJourney – IT Learning & Career Platform"

---

MAIN PURPOSE

Create a mobile app that teaches:

1. IT career paths and job roles
2. Programming languages
3. Certification preparation
4. Interactive coding practice
5. Career roadmaps and skill progression

The app should help beginners progress to advanced professional levels.
